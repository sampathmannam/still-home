# Privacy

Still Home has no Internet permission, accounts, advertising, analytics, crash
reporting service, tracking SDKs, or cloud backup. It does not operate a server.

It reads launchable app labels and icons visible to the current Android user for
its app drawer. App search takes place in memory and is not saved. The appearance
choice is saved locally in Android app preferences. Uninstalling clears that choice.

The network-state permission allows a local check of the active network's VPN
transport. The app also displays Android's security-patch value and whether the
system reports a secure screen lock. These checks are status displays, not proof
that the device is secure or anonymous.

Opening another app, a store listing, or a website hands control to another app.
Its privacy policy and permissions apply. Cloud AI services receive submitted
content. This launcher does not modify their privacy settings.

This policy describes Still Home, not the operating system, keyboard, app store,
VPN provider, or other installed applications. No personal setup logs or device
identifiers are included in the public source repository.
